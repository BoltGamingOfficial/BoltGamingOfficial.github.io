Community Rules

Twenty rules. Read once, keep forever.

Clear lines, real consequences. Break these and staff act — no warning owed, no debate required.

🤝
01

Be kind to everyone

Basic courtesy is not optional. Treat every member the way you'd like to be treated.

🚫
02

No racism or hate

No hate toward anyone based on race, religion, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or identity. Zero tolerance.

🗳️
03

No politics

This isn't the place for it. Discussions about governments, elections, or ideologies will be removed without exception.

🔞
04

No NSFW content

No porn, gory scenes, or explicit material — in messages, links, or media files. This includes avatars, banners, status updates, and usernames.

🖼️
05

No stickers, GIFs, or media that incite hatred

Insults, hate symbols, or images used to belittle anyone — even as a joke — are prohibited.

🎩
06

Be respectful toward users and staff

Staff enforce the rules for everyone's benefit. Insulting them while they're doing their job is not tolerated.

🛡️
07

No harassment, raids, or threats

Do not harass any users, raid the server, or send threats. Threats of any kind are taken seriously and result in an immediate ban.

🔒
08

No doxxing or disclosure of personal information

Never share anyone's real name, address, phone number, place of work, photos, or private information — not even your own, unless you want it to be public knowledge.

🔐
09

Do not disclose private conversations

Screenshots of direct messages or ticket contents posted in public channels without consent will be removed. Report them instead of making them public.

📢
10

No spam or unauthorized advertising

No repetitive messages, no unsolicited direct messages regarding your server, product, or service. Promotion is allowed exclusively through sponsors.

💿
11

Discussion ≠ distribution

Reverse engineering, DRM, cracking theory, and how piracy works are welcome in the appropriate channels. Sharing pirated files, crack links, torrents, serial codes, or direct downloads is not. Discuss these topics, but do not distribute them here.

🎣
12

No scam or phishing links

Programs designed to steal tokens, fake Nitro offers, malware droppers, and any links intended to steal accounts or data will result in an immediate and permanent ban. No exceptions, no appeals.

🛟
13

Do not glorify self-harm

Discussing mental health is fine. Encouraging, instructing, or glorifying suicide, self-harm, or eating disorders is not. Reach out to #ticket if you need support.

👥
14

Do not circumvent bans

If you've been banned, creating an alternate account to return will extend the ban and result in the alternate account being reported to Discord. Appeals must be submitted via #ticket.

📌
15

Stay on topic

Each channel has a specific purpose. Off-topic posts belong in #general. Read the channel description before posting.

⚖️
16

Respect Discord's Terms of Service

Discord's rules apply here as well. If something could get your account banned on Discord, don't do it here.

📮
17

Staff decisions are final

Discuss issues in #ticket, not in public channels. Public arguments about moderation will also result in removal. Use the appropriate channel.

🔞
18

You must be at least 13 years old

This is the minimum age required by Discord. If we discover you're younger, you'll be kicked — with no chance of appeal. This rule is essential for the server's survival.

🚨
19

Illegal content is reported

Credible threats, child sexual abuse material (CSAM), drug sales, and actual criminal activity will be removed AND reported to Discord Trust & Safety. We cooperate fully.

🎭
20

Impersonation can have consequences

Impersonating a staff member for the purpose of fraud will be punished. We recommend that you do not impersonate anyone for your own safety.
