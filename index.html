Community Rules

Read once. Keep forever.

Short list, clear consequences. Break these and staff act — no warning owed, no debate required.

🤝

Be nice to everyone

Basic decency is not optional. Treat every member the way you want to be treated.

🚫

No racism or hate

No hate against anyone for their race, religion, ethnicity, gender, sexuality, disability, or identity. Zero tolerance.

🗳️

No politics

This is not the place. Debates about governments, elections, or ideologies are removed without discussion.

🔞

No NSFW content

No porn, gore, or explicit material — in messages, links, or media. Avatars, banners, statuses and nicknames included.

🖼️

No hateful stickers, GIFs or media

Slurs, hate symbols, or imagery used to demean anyone — even as a joke — is banned.

🎩

Be respectful to members and staff

Staff enforce the rules for everyone's benefit. Insulting them for doing their job is not tolerated.

🛡️

No harassment, raids, or threats

Do not harass any member, raid the server, or send threats. Threats of any kind are taken seriously and result in an immediate ban.

🔒

No doxxing or personal info

Never share anyone's real name, address, phone, workplace, photos, or private data — not even your own if you don't want it public.

🔐

No leaking private conversations

Screenshots of DMs or ticket content posted in public channels without consent get removed. Report instead of exposing.

📢

No spam or unauthorized ads

No repeated messages, no unsolicited DMs about your server, product, or service. Promotion goes through #sponsor only.

💿

Discussion ≠ distribution

Reverse engineering, DRM, cracking theory, and how piracy works are welcome in the right channels. Sharing pirated files, crack links, torrents, serials, or direct downloads is not. Talk about it — don't ship it here.

🎣

No scam or phishing links

Token grabbers, fake Nitro, malware droppers, and any link designed to steal accounts or data are an instant permanent ban. No exceptions, no appeals.

🛟

No glorifying self-harm

Talking about mental health is fine. Encouraging, instructing, or glorifying suicide, self-harm, or eating disorders is not. Reach out in #ticket if you need support.

👥

No ban evasion

If you're banned, creating an alt to return extends the ban and gets the alt reported to Discord. Appeals go through #ticket.

📌

Stay on topic

Each channel has a purpose. Off-topic posts go to #general. Read the channel description before posting.

⚖️

Follow Discord's Terms of Service

Discord's rules apply here too. If something would get your account banned on Discord, don't bring it here.

📮

Staff decisions are final

Argue in #ticket, not in public channels. Public drama about moderation gets you removed too. Use the proper channel.

🎂

Must be 13+ to be here

Discord's minimum age. If we find out you're younger, you're removed — no appeal. This one keeps the server alive.

🚨

Illegal content gets reported

Credible threats, CSAM, drug sales, and actual criminal activity get removed AND reported to Discord Trust & Safety. We cooperate fully.
